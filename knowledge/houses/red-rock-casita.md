# Red Rock Casita house rules

Home: red-rock-casita
Revision: 2026-11-16

## Pets

Pets are not allowed.

## Check-in

Check in from 4:00 pm local time.

## Check-out

Check out by 11:00 am local time.

## Parties

Parties and events are not allowed.

## Smoking

No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property.

## Parking

Free parking for one car in the marked space. Do not use the neighboring spaces.
