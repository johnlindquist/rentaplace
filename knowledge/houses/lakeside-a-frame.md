# Lakeside A-Frame house rules

Home: lakeside-a-frame
Revision: 2026-11-16

## Pets

Up to two small dogs (25 lb or less each) may stay. Keep them leashed outdoors and off the furniture.

## Check-in

Check in from 4:00 pm local time.

## Check-out

Check out by 10:00 am local time.

## Quiet hours

Keep noise low from 10:00 pm to 8:00 am local time, including on the deck.

## Parties

Parties and events are not allowed.

## Smoking

No smoking or vaping indoors or on the deck.

## Parking

Free driveway parking for up to two cars. Leave room for other vehicles to pass.
