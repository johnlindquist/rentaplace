# Pricing expectations

Scenario checks, in the lab's form: Do is the plain question, Expect what must come back, Never what must not.

## Weekend means Friday and Saturday
Do (Pricing): Is Sunday night charged at the weekend price?
Expect (Pricing): says no, only Friday and Saturday nights are weekend nights
Never (Pricing): says Sunday is charged at the weekend price

## A surcharge is not defined
Do (Pricing): How much is the holiday surcharge?
Expect (Pricing): says a surcharge is not defined or not decided yet
Never (Pricing): states an amount or a percentage for the surcharge
